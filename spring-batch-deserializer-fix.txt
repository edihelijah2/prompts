In this Spring Boot 4 / Spring Batch 6.0.5 project, jobs fail on restart with `Failed to deserialize object` because the default `DefaultExecutionContextSerializer` rejects custom classes that aren't on its `ObjectInputFilter` allow-list (CVE-2026-47878 hardening). Add a custom `ExecutionContextSerializer` bean, wired into the job repository, that extends the default allow-list patterns with only our packages (`com.example.etl.model.**`) and keeps the trailing `!*` reject-all. Then verify that an existing context containing `EtlCheckpoint` deserializes, and that classes outside the allow-list are still rejected.
